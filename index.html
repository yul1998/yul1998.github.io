01 · About简介

Building reliable bridges between vision and language.连接视觉与语言，构建可信的多模态智能。

My work explores the foundations and applications of multimodal learning. I am particularly interested in models that remain effective under distribution shifts, noisy correspondence, or missing modalities—and in using causal ideas to make their decisions more robust and interpretable. 我的工作探索多模态学习的基础方法与应用。我尤其关注模型在分布偏移、噪声对应与模态缺失条件下的有效性，并尝试借助因果思想提升模型决策的稳健性与可解释性。
